Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026.

Your files

Files are converted locally in your browser. They are not uploaded, stored or seen by us.

Cookies

Only if you click Accept, we set two first-party cookies for one year: cv_id, a random ID for your device, and cv_n, the number of times you visited. They are used only to show your visit count. They contain no name or email and are not shared or sold. If you decline, no cookies are set. Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage, and you can change it any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Third parties

There are no ads and no analytics. Some conversions (MP3, PDF, DOCX, video) load an open-source library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com or cdn.jsdelivr.net when you use them, which may see your IP address.
